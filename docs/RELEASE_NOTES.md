## New in 1.0.66

PC only. The phone app stays **DRAUPNE Flight 2.3.0-pc.52** (in this release too).

### Flight time and range left
- **From the drone's battery:** a full pack counts as 23 min and 14 km; what is left follows the pack's charge.
- **STANAG page:** FLIGHT and RANGE beside the batteries.
- **FLEET:** click a drone - on the GPS line, e.g. `13 MIN / 8.1 KM`. Yellow when the pack is low.

### Mind
- An estimate from the voltage. It reads low while the drone flies (the pack sags under load).
