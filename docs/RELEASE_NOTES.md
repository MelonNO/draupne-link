## New in 1.0.40

PC only: the phone app stays **DRAUPNE Flight 2.3.0-pc.31**.

- **Drone setup, one card:** **Betaflight settings** is one point with the receiver, stick delay and battery lines, then **GPS**, **SWITCHES** and **FAILSAFE** as sub-points with a small light each, and CONFIRM at the bottom; the motors and props stay their own point. The rows no longer repeat what the choices beside them say. Each switch is one line: its choice, and beside it its state in colour - *Set.*, *Set on Confirm.* or what is wrong - not "AUX3 - written with Write" under a PREARM list that already shows AUX3. The lines sit in aligned columns, and a row with nothing to say has no empty line under its title. *What will change* and *Betaflight settings for DRAUPNE Link* are one row, **Betaflight settings**. **The flow is: set everything, then CONFIRM.** One CONFIRM button (the cell count's own Confirm is gone: it counts) writes every setting on the page to the flight controller, backed up first; until then every row shows an empty ring, with *Set on Confirm.* where Betaflight differs, and once everything is there and confirmed the rows turn green. Any change afterwards brings CONFIRM back. Until CONFIRM is pressed the Betaflight settings' light and its sub-points' lights are **red**.
- **One place per mode:** the flight modes this setup manages (Angle, Horizon, GPS position hold, GPS rescue) are taken off every other switch when the flight mode switch is written, so none sits on two channels (a drone came out of the setup with a mode on AUX2 and AUX5); with the switch left as it is, a mode found on two switches is reported on the row.
- **GPS, asked first:** a **GPS** row above the switches - *No* or *Yes*. With *Yes*, **COMPASS** (No / Yes), the GPS's **UART** (UART3 by default; the list follows the board once Betaflight is read) and **CALIBRATE COMPASS** slide out under it, and so does the **GPS RESCUE** switch under the switches; GPS position hold and GPS rescue appear in the flight-mode lists and GPS rescue in the failsafe list. With *No*, nothing GPS is shown or written (a GPS mode or failsafe chosen before becomes Angle / Leave as it is). Not answered yet: the page takes Betaflight's own answer when it reads the drone.
- **GPS written like the rest:** with *Yes*, CONFIRM turns Betaflight's GPS on, puts the GPS function on the UART chosen (taken off any other port) and turns on `gps_auto_baud` so the module is found at whatever rate it talks; the row then says *GPS found*, or what to check when it doesn't answer. **COMPASS** *Yes* sets `mag_hardware` AUTO (*No* sets NONE, as Betaflight Configurator's Magnetometer switch does); the row says whether Betaflight sees one and whether it is calibrated.
- **Flight modes side by side:** SWITCH, LOW, MIDDLE and HIGH each with its name over its list, and what the line has to say (a mode this Betaflight is too old for) under them, across the card.
- **No nagging:** the "calibrate the radio again" warning after a switch change is gone (the first-time setup still shows the radio step again, quietly); with a GPS, Betaflight's `gps_rescue_allow_arming_without_fix` is set ON with the rest, so there is no "won't arm until the GPS has a fix" note either; a compass still to calibrate and modes left on a switch left as it is are said in plain text, not as warnings - a point whose settings are set turns green.
- **Motor test: fix it there, not just see it:** **Reorder motors** spins each motor in turn while you click the place in the picture where it is; Betaflight's motor order is then set to match (saved, it restarts). **Spin direction** under the picture sets each motor's direction in its ESC - Normal or Reversed, saved in the ESC (DShot ESCs; others: swap two motor wires). **Props in / props out** now sets Betaflight's motor direction too, after asking, and the picture always shows what Betaflight has. Props off, battery in. The drone's picture is about twice as large: the props switch and Reorder motors sit left of it, Spin direction right of it.
- **Calibration's 3D radio, toggles that work like the real ones:** each toggle's round collar stays on the radio and only the flat paddle tilts, hinged at the collar's top, from one end of its throw to the other (before, collar and paddle tipped together about a point inside the bushing and the paddle hardly left its first position).
- **Calibration: letting go of a button is not the next switch.** A button pressed for one AUX - and anything the radio reports along with it - counts for nothing in the next switch step until it is back where it was; before, a radio that reports a press on two inputs had the release taken as the next AUX's switch.
- **Compass calibration:** CALIBRATE COMPASS opens a window of its own (like the motor test): START, then turn the drone round every axis for 30 s while two discs - from above and from the side - fill with dots where the compass has pointed; at the end the offsets are read back and kept, and the row says *Compass found and calibrated*.
