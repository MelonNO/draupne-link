## New in 1.0.60

With the phone app **DRAUPNE Flight 2.3.0-pc.47**: update the phones too.

### New
- **DEMO mode** (SETUP > Demo mode): every virtual drone and scene up at once, nothing in the way - fly, observe, or watch a virtual pilot PC as SPECTATOR (a card switches it to observing and back). FLEET MANAGEMENT sets up and starts a scenario. SETUP > End demo takes it all away. Replaces the Overwatch demo.
- **Overwatch clips play as one video:** the pictures before the alert and the video on one clock, the bar goes anywhere in it.
- **Detection areas: a button by the video**, under MUTE.

### Changed
- **Map and FLEET signs by mode:** arrow = piloted, diamond = standby, moon = deep sleep, eye = overwatch. A queued mode shows as a yellow arrow to its sign, with how long ago it was queued.
- **FLEET MANAGEMENT:** PILOTED, STANDBY, OVERWATCH, DEEP SLEEP on top - the current one grey (a press shakes: not possible), a queued one yellow (press again to drop it); CLIPS and a CHECK EVERY list below.
- **LINK card:** DEEP SLEEP and OVERWATCH always shown, the current one grey.
- **Picking a phone** on the map or in FLEET picks it in the PHONE list (not connected), and the other way round.
- **REGAINING CONTROL counts down in seconds.**
- **Phone:** silent and without vibration while the app works (asks for Do Not Disturb access once).
- **Tour:** MAP TOOLS moves on once pressed; PREVIOUS FLIGHTS' box beside its window; the scenes' backdrop as the finale's; more round the finale's patrol.

### Fixed
- **The tour no longer freezes at MOVING AROUND.**
- **STANDBY from the map** works for the phone this PC flies.
- **Standby no longer shows the deep-sleep sign.**
- **Phone:** the start animation no longer jumps at its end.
- **Overwatch:** a camera that refused the detection's picture during an event no longer stays blind; the log says when an overwatching phone's camera gives no pictures.
