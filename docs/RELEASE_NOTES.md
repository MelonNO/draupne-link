## New in 1.0.57

With **DRAUPNE Flight 2.3.0-pc.45**. Update the PC app, then the phone (UPDATE PHONE APP, drone disarmed).

Not yet tried on a drone or real phone: bench-test with props off first.

### Fixed
- **Window freeze** after a while of video and on coming back from alt-tab.
- **Tour:** the spectator part no longer gets stuck; closing with the X removes all tour drones from the map and fleet.

### New
- **Muted observation tiles light up** when their drone hears something (phone pc.45).
- **Every map is auto:** outside Norway, topo and paper show the world topo. No AUTO entry.
- **Tour:** you select each mode yourself; realistic tactical scenes (drone team, convoy, patrol, checkpoint, mortar, vehicle hide) in a simulated-memory look; no beeper noise; new MUTE AND LISTEN step.
