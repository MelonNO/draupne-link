## New in 1.0.40

PC only: the phone app stays **DRAUPNE Flight 2.3.0-pc.31**.

- **Drone setup, less to read:** the rows no longer repeat what the choices beside them say. A switch line says *Set.*, *Written with Write.* or what is wrong - not "AUX3 - written with Write" under a PREARM list that already shows AUX3. *What will change* and *Betaflight settings for DRAUPNE Link* are one row, **Betaflight settings**, with the WRITE button on it; the lines marked *Written with Write* across the page are what it writes.
- **GPS, asked first:** a **GPS** row above the switches - *No* or *Yes*. With *Yes*, **COMPASS** (No / Yes), the GPS's **UART** (UART3 by default; the list follows the board once Betaflight is read) and **CALIBRATE COMPASS** slide out under it, and so does the **GPS RESCUE** switch under the switches; GPS position hold and GPS rescue appear in the flight-mode lists and GPS rescue in the failsafe list. With *No*, nothing GPS is shown or written (a GPS mode or failsafe chosen before becomes Angle / Leave as it is). Not answered yet: the page takes Betaflight's own answer when it reads the drone.
- **GPS written like the rest:** with *Yes*, WRITE turns Betaflight's GPS on, puts the GPS function on the UART chosen (taken off any other port) and turns on `gps_auto_baud` so the module is found at whatever rate it talks; the row then says *GPS found*, or what to check when it doesn't answer. **COMPASS** *Yes* sets `mag_hardware` AUTO (*No* sets NONE, as Betaflight Configurator's Magnetometer switch does); the row says whether Betaflight sees one and whether it is calibrated.
- **Compass calibration:** CALIBRATE COMPASS opens a window of its own (like the motor test): START, then turn the drone round every axis for 30 s while two discs - from above and from the side - fill with dots where the compass has pointed; at the end the offsets are read back and kept, and the row says *Compass found and calibrated*.
