## New in 1.0.41

PC only: the phone app stays **DRAUPNE Flight 2.3.0-pc.31**. Install it on the pilot PC and the spectator PCs alike.

- **Spectators no longer slow the pilot down.** Relaying the picture to a spectator goes out through the pilot PC's own upload; at the sharp 8 Mbit/s that clogged it, and the pilot's ping to the drone - the sticks - went from about 50 ms to 300-700 ms within seconds. Now, while anyone watches, AUTO stays on the normal picture (4 Mbit/s at most) and leaves the sharp one; a SHARP picked in VIDEO stays.
- **The spectators' picture pauses until the pilot's link has recovered,** not for a fixed 3 s: before, it came back after 3 s with the ping still high, paused again a second later, and the spectator got one second of picture in every four. It now goes on once the pilot's ping has been back to normal for 3 s; a pause that follows soon after another lasts at least twice as long (3 s up to 30 s). The pilot's usual ping is taken over the last minute, so a long spell of congestion no longer becomes "the usual".
- **A spectator waits through a pause** it was told about (up to 15 s) instead of giving up after 3 s and taking 5-10 s to join again.
- **The spectator's OSD mirrors the pilot PC's.** The pilot PC sends what its own OSD warns of - RC OFF and why (NO RADIO, THROTTLE DOWN ...), RADIO FROZEN, ARM SWITCH NOT READ, STANDBY, STICKS LATE, RELAY, LINK STALL, LINK LOST and why, and the stick boxes - and the spectator shows that, not its own PC's state. Needs 1.0.41 on the pilot PC; with an older pilot PC the spectator shows what it did before.
- **A phone left in deep sleep can be woken after DISCONNECT.** Before, once the pilot disconnected from a sleeping phone, nothing answered its check-ins and the PC forgot it was asleep - there was no WAKE. Now the LINK card keeps it: *MobileAlpha is in DEEP SLEEP*, when it was last heard and its battery, when it checks next, and **WAKE**. Until WAKE the PC answers each check-in "sleep on", so the phone closes its listening window early and saves power; WAKE connects to it and it comes back at its next check. CONNECT to it without WAKE keeps it asleep (WAKE then brings it back), as before the disconnect. It is remembered across a restart of the PC app, and a phone woken by hand on the phone is noticed.
