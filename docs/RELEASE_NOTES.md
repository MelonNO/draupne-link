## New in 1.0.34

Phone app **DRAUPNE Flight 2.3.0-pc.26** is unchanged: no phone update needed.

- **Video mode:** the PC now tells the phone its video profile with the first settings of every session, the normal one included. The phone keeps the profile of the session before, so a phone left in bad coverage mode (key frames twice, parity packets, H.264) stayed in it under a PC that took it for normal - until the next ROBUST / SHARP change. Check the version in the title bar, then try SHARP and ROBUST once each: the log says what each does.
- **Tour:** the first step tells you every button can be pressed twice (the second press closes or undoes the first; NEXT stays unlocked). EXIT TOUR is a small cross in the top right corner of the notice; NEXT spans the box.
- **Guide:** the *Connect DRAUPNE Flight* step no longer scrolls down by itself while its checks update. The *Sleep before take-off* step is gone: the tour's SLEEP BUTTONS step covers it.
