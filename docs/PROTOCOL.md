# DroidDrone protocol notes (protocol version 9, app 2.3.0, upstream commit 473608d)

These notes were worked out from the upstream source. File references are relative to `upstream/`.

## Topology
- **Flight app** (phone on the drone) talks MSP or MAVLink to the FC over USB OTG or UART, and encodes camera video.
- **Control app** (phone with the radio) shows video and OSD and sends RC. **This project replaces it with the PC.**
- **Server** (`Server/`, optional Java relay) is for when neither side has a reachable IP. Viewers can also connect through it.
- **Direct mode:** the Flight phone listens on UDP 6286 and the controller connects to its IP. A phone on 4G has no reachable IP, so direct mode then needs a VPN.
- **Over-server mode:** both sides connect out to the relay (`Server/src/.../Udp.java`). The relay echoes each `Connect` itself and forwards packets between drone (client type 0) and controller (type 1), so the controller code is the same in both modes. The relay knows clients by IP:port.

## Transport (`Common/.../UdpCommon.java`, `UdpSender.java`, `ReceiverBuffer.java`)
- UDP, default port 6286, packets of at most 1100 bytes, big-endian (Java `DataOutputStream`). Strings use `writeUTF`.
- Byte 0 is the packet type (0–26).
- **Numbered packets** (`isPacketNumbered`) carry an int16 sequence number after the type. The receiver delivers them in order and asks for gaps with `RequestPackets`.
- Some numbered packets are **ACKed** with `PacketReceived` (`isSendPacketReceived`). The sender resends them about every 1.2 × ping until ACKed or expired (1 s).
- Video, audio and RC frames are unnumbered and fire-and-forget.
- The link is considered lost after about 4000 ticks of `processTimer()` (about 4 s at the 1 ms loop) with no packets.
- **Patched copies (DRAUPNE Link 1.0.33 / DRAUPNE Flight pc.26, `flight-app/overlay/Common`, compiled into both apps):** the resend timer of a session is cancelled when the next session starts (upstream's timer task cancelled the *new* session's timer, so from the first reconnect on no acknowledged packet was ever resent, on either side); no empty `RequestPackets`; the client side (the PC, or the phone over a relay) takes the far side's answer to its own `Connect` while it still counts the old session as up (a new session has begun there). The server side keeps upstream's rule.
- **pc.31 / DRAUPNE Link 1.0.39 transport fixes (both apps):** a timed-out session is never revived by a stray packet (`addPacket` checks `isConnected()`), and with no session the phone takes nothing but a keyed `Connect`; a gap in the numbered packets is waited for 1.5 × ping (at least 30 ms, at least 4 packets counted) with `RequestPackets` before it is skipped, the next packet found across the wrap, and a late copy of a skipped packet is not acknowledged; `Connect` is no longer resent by the acknowledgement timer (the PC has its own schedule); the phone ignores a repeat `Connect` from the pilot within 1 s of a session's start; `sendPacket` tries once and logs at most every 2 s. No session id: packets of an old session still in flight are made rarer, not impossible.
- **The pilot's own re-Connect (pc.26):** a keyed `Connect` from the connected PC's own address and port starts a new session on the phone at once (the PC counts the link lost after 2 s of silence; before, the Connect was dropped until the phone's 4 s timer ran out). Another PC takes the session over only after 10 s of silence from the pilot (was 1 s), and never while the flight controller reports armed. pc.31: the same rule after the session's 4 s timeout too (before, any keyed PC took it then, armed or not), and "armed" counts unknown as armed (no fresh status, FC re-initialising, mode ids unread, MAVLink).
- **After a loss (1.0.33):** the PC sends `Connect` every 150 ms for 5 s (then 500 ms), the `Config` every 250 ms the moment the phone answers, keeps the flight controller's state (FcInfo, mode names) for 60 s and the video config, and keeps sending stick frames and pings whether or not the phone is heard. Once READY again it asks FcInfo and BoxNames once more, and asks for the stream again only if no video fragment came within 1.5 s of the link's return (the phone's stream usually never stopped), or when telemetry comes but no video for 3 s.
- Ping/Pong: the controller sends `Ping(toEndPoint=true, timeMs)` every 500 ms, and the end point echoes it as `Pong`.

## Handshake (Control `MainActivity.startConnectionThread`)
1. Every 500 ms: `Connect` = `[8][int16 -1][clientType u8][key UTF][version int16]`. Client type: 0 = flight, 1 = controller, 2 = viewer.
   The Flight side (`ReceiverBuffer` with isServer = true) checks the key and echoes the `Connect` back, which connects the controller too.
2. Every 500 ms until answered: `Config` (layout in `Control/.../Udp.sendConfig` and `Flight/.../Config.processReceivedConfig`).
   The Flight app replies `ConfigReceived`, or `VersionMismatch` if the version differs from 9.
3. `StartVideo(isHevcSupported bool)` every 2.5 s until a `VideoInitialFrame` arrives.
4. Requests for `FcInfo`, `OsdConfig`, `BoxIds`, `BatteryConfig`. Each is an empty packet of that type, and the answer comes back with the same type.

## RC
- `RcFrame` = `[24][count u8][count × int16]`, with 4–18 channels at 1000–2000, sent at `rcRefreshRate` Hz (default 25).
- The Flight app's only use of `rcRefreshRate` is a minimum gap in `Msp.setRawRc` (and `Mavlink`): frames arriving within `1000 / rate / 2` ms of the previous one are **dropped**. DRAUPNE Link announces 255, the most the byte holds (a 1 ms window), and sends at its own rate.
- Channel order is **roll, pitch, yaw, throttle, then AUX1…** - Betaflight's rcmap index order (the order the FC's channel map maps *from*), not AETR. The Flight app applies the FC's `rxmap` (`Msp.processRxMap`) before `MSP_SET_RAW_RC`, and only when the frame has at least 8 channels, so DRAUPNE Link pads every frame to 8 (`ControlLink.padForRxMap`, missing AUX at 1000).
- The Flight RC thread only forwards frames it actually receives. If no frame arrives, nothing is sent to the FC, and Betaflight's RX-loss failsafe triggers.
- **Idle sticks (DRAUPNE Link 1.0.44 + DRAUPNE Flight 2.3.0-pc.33):** `IDLE_STICKS [0x78][count u8][count × int16]`, unnumbered, once a second to a phone of pc.33 or newer, from the pilot's session only: throttle 1000, roll/pitch/yaw 1500, the arm switch at a value outside every ARM range (`ArmSwitch`; none sent while it isn't known), the BEEPER channel (AUX14 from 1.0.49, AUX12 before) 1000, the other AUX as the radio has them. The phone keeps the last ones while it runs and sends them to the FC every 50 ms while no `RcFrame` has been taken for 300 ms and the FC's last status - read at least 100 ms after that frame, at most 2.5 s old - says disarmed. Never while armed or unknown: the RX-loss failsafe above is unchanged in the air.
- **BEEPER (1.0.44):** every frame carries at least 18 channels (16 before 1.0.49); AUX14 (index 17; AUX12, index 15, before 1.0.49) is the app's BEEPER button (2000 while held, else 1000), where Drone setup puts Betaflight's BEEPER mode.
- **Sequence byte (DRAUPNE Link + DRAUPNE Flight 2.3.0-pc.19 on):** the PC appends one byte to the frame, `[24][count u8][count × int16][seq u8]`, a sequence number counted up per frame and wrapping 0..255. The phone accepts both lengths and drops a frame whose sequence is not newer than the last one it wrote (frames arrive bunched and out of order over 4G; a frame late is a frame wrong). The PC adds the byte only once the phone has reported a version of pc.19 or newer (record 0x40F1): older Flight apps refuse a frame whose length is not `count × 2` (`Udp.processData`, case RcFrame). `ControlLink.rcFrame` builds it, `RC_SEQ_MIN_PC_BUILD` is the cut.
- **16-bit sequence (pc.26 on):** to a phone app of pc.26 or newer the PC sends two bytes, `[seq u16]` (0..65535): with one byte a frame 128 or more behind looked newer and the fresh frames after it were dropped. The phone also starts the rule over after 300 ms without a frame taken. The `PHONE_STATE` record carries the low byte.
- **Send time and lateness limit (pc.31 on):** `[24][count u8][count × int16][seq u16][sentMs u16][maxLagCs u8]` - the PC's monotonic ms (low 16 bits) when the frame left, and the lateness past which the phone drops it in 10 ms steps (0 = never; the PC's `rc.maxLagMs`, default 0). The phone's lag = the frame's (arrival - sentMs) over the smallest such value in its two 10 s windows (only differences count, so the two clocks needn't agree); reset with every new session. It reports it as record `0x40F6`. After the 300 ms reset a 16-bit frame is taken only if newer than the last one, anything after 1 s (pc.31; 8-bit frames keep the 300 ms rule).

## Video (milestone 2)
- Android `MediaCodec` encoder: H.265, falling back to H.264. I-frame interval 0.5 s. Bitrate up to `bitrateLimit`, adapted by `ChangeBitRate(increase bool)` from the controller.
- `VideoInitialFrame` = `[0][int16 num][width i16][height i16][isHevc bool][isFrontCamera bool][codec config bytes (VPS/SPS/PPS)]`.
- `VideoFrame`/`KeyFrame` = `[1|2][frameNum i16][offset i32][frameSize i32 if offset==0][bytes]`. Frames are split across packets and put back together by offset (`Control/.../FrameFragments.java`).
- The controller lowers the bitrate when frames are lost or late, and raises it again after 10 s without losses (`Udp.processBitRateChange`).
- **DRAUPNE Link's video packets (PC to phone, unnumbered; older apps drop what they don't know):** `KEY_FRAME_REQUEST [0x6A]` (pc.13); `VIDEO_PROFILE [0x6B][flags]` (pc.15: bit 0 = key frames twice; pc.26: bit 1 = a parity packet per group of 8 packets, bit 2 = encode H.264; 1.0.34: sent with the first config of every session, the normal profile too - the phone keeps the flags of the session before); `SET_BITRATE [0x72][step u8]` (pc.26: the encoder's step outright - the phone's table is 0.15, 0.25, 0.5, 1, 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 15, 20 Mbit/s; a step above the PC's limit is clamped, a raise is ignored for 2 s after the phone's own emergency brake).
- **DRAUPNE names (1.0.35, phone app pc.28), on the link port, each starting with the link key (another key: dropped):** `NAME_QUERY [0x73][key UTF]` from a PC to every online Tailscale device (who are you?); `NAME_REPLY [0x74][key UTF][kind u8: 0 phone, 1 PC][name UTF][device UTF][version UTF]` back to the asker (the name "" while a phone has none); `NAME_ASSIGN [0x75][key UTF][name UTF]` from the master PC (the pilot PC with the highest letter online) to a nameless phone, which keeps the name and answers with it. A PC names itself with the first PC letter it has not seen. The open link answers queries on its socket; otherwise a PC answers from the link port.
- **Parity (phone to PC):** `VIDEO_PARITY [0x6C][frameNum i16][frameSize i32][keyFrame u8][XOR of the frame's packet payloads, each zero-padded]` (pc.15, one per frame in bad coverage mode); `VIDEO_PARITY_GROUP [0x71][frameNum i16][frameSize i32][keyFrame u8][group u8][XOR of packets group*8 .. group*8+7]` (pc.26, when profile bit 1 is set). Packet i of a frame is at offset `1087 + (i - 1) * 1091` (i > 0): payloads are 1087 bytes for the first packet and 1091 for the rest.
- **Frame numbers:** 1..32767; the phone wraps to 2 (pc.26; apps before wrap to 1), so a key frame numbered 1 always means a (re)started stream. The PC's `FrameAssembler` tolerates the old wrap.
- **The tiers (1.0.33):** normal (the set size, 30 fps), bad coverage mode (640x360, up to 1 Mbit/s, profile bits 0-2) and the survival tier (480x270 at 15 fps, up to 250 kbit/s, the phone's 150 kbit/s step as the floor). The size and frame rate go as a `Config`, the rest as the profile. The PC asks for H.264 (`StartVideo(false)`, profile bit 2) in bad coverage mode and under: FFmpeg's H.264 decoder conceals a damaged frame from the one before, its H.265 decoder shows the damage. Above normal (1.0.34) the sharp tier: the sharp size and bitrate limit from the PC's settings (1920x1080, up to 8 Mbit/s; the `Config`'s bitrate-limit byte carries the tier's limit, so the phone allows its 8 Mbit/s step, index 7). AUTO earns it after 20 s calm at the normal top bitrate and leaves it at a stall, a relay or 5 s under the normal top. A phone (pc.27) without a small 16:9 size takes the smallest size of any shape with at least the asked pixels for bad coverage mode and the survival tier (640x480 for 640x360 and 480x270), and the PC letterboxes it.
- `GetVideoConfig [4]` (numbered, acknowledged) makes the phone send its cached `VideoInitialFrame` again without restarting anything: the PC asks when a restarted stream's config hasn't come within 300 ms, or a key frame decodes to no picture.

## Live audio (DRAUPNE Link 1.0.45 + DRAUPNE Flight 2.3.0-pc.34)
- **Upstream's packets, unchanged:** `AudioInitialFrame [12][num i16][sampleRate i32][channels u8][Android PCM encoding u8][AAC AudioSpecificConfig]` (numbered, acknowledged; sent when the encoder starts, and again on `GetVideoConfig` or the flag below) and `AudioFrame [13][one raw AAC-LC frame, no ADTS header, 1024 samples]` (unnumbered, fire and forget). pc.34 records mono (its `AudioSource`), 48 kHz, so the config is `0x11 0x88`; upstream encoded only at a video bitrate of 2 Mbit/s and up, pc.34 at any.
- **`AUDIO [0x79][on u8][kbps u8, 16-64][flags u8]`** (PC to phone, unnumbered, `PcAudio`): sent by the pilot PC once the phone has said a version of pc.34 or newer, at every change and again every second. `on` = the PC wants the sound; the phone treats an "on" as wanted for 3 s (a PC gone without a word doesn't keep its microphone running). Flags bit 0 = send the `AudioInitialFrame` again (the PC has frames but missed the config). The PC asks off after its MUTE has been on for 10 s, on again at the unmute; it always asks 48 kbit/s. Older phone apps drop the packet - the PC doesn't send it to them. Upstream's `Config` audio fields stay off (`sendAudioStream` false): the phone's `isSendAudioStream` paths are untouched.
- **The phone's gate (`Udp.audioGateRun`, every 50 ms):** the microphone and encoder run while the PC's "on" is fresh, the flight controller reads DISARMED in a fresh status (`FcArmedState`: unknown counts as armed; no FC connected counts as disarmed unless it was armed within the last minute), the phone is in normal power mode (not STANDBY or a sleep) and a PC session is up. The sending stops at once (the sender checks the gate before each frame); the encoder and microphone are stopped and started on the video starter thread. A failed start is tried again every 2 s. The encoder's input goes through a 120 Hz first-order high-pass filter (wind).
- **`PHONE_STATE` flags (pc.34):** bit 5 the sound is on, bit 6 held back (asked for, but the FC isn't known disarmed or the phone is in standby), bit 7 the microphone or AAC encoder didn't start.
- **The PC:** frames go from the receive thread into `LiveAudio`'s queue (and the recording's); its own thread at the lowest priority decodes (FFmpeg AAC), keeps a jitter buffer (plays from 200 ms in hand, fills to 200 ms again after running dry, cuts back to 200 ms past 450 ms) and writes to the default sound device (about 60 ms buffer). MUTE flushes the device's buffer. Alert beeps lower the stream to 20 %.
- **Spectators:** the pilot PC relays `AudioInitialFrame` and `AudioFrame` in its stream like the video, and puts the config into the join snapshot. A spectator never sends `AUDIO`; its MUTE is its own.
- **Recordings:** an AAC track (the frames copied) in `flight-....mkv` and `flight-...-osd.mkv`, timestamps counted per frame from the first one's arrival on the video's clock (again from the arrival when the count is 250 ms behind it: a pause, or frames lost). `max_interleave_delta` 1 s.

## Observation (DRAUPNE Link 1.0.48 + DRAUPNE Flight 2.3.0-pc.37)
A PC watches up to six idle drones at once and flies none of them; CONNECT on a tile opens an ordinary pilot connection. Codes 0x50-0x57 and record 0x40FE-0x40FF belong to it (0x53-0x57 and 0x40FF unused, reserved). Shared code: `Common/.../Observe` (both apps). Sent only to a phone that said pc.37 or newer in its `NAME_REPLY`; an older app drops the packets.
- **`OBSERVE [0x50][key UTF][width u16][height u16][fps u8][maxKbps u16][flags u8][detection u8]`** PC to phone, on the link port from the PC's own socket for that phone (not its link port), unnumbered, once a second and at once when the tile's picture changes or it needs a config. The grid's picture is 640x360, 15 fps, 1000 kbit/s; the selected or enlarged tile asks for the PC's NORMAL (its camera size, 30 fps, its bitrate limit). Flags: bit 0 the sound wanted (off after the tile has been muted 10 s), bit 1 send the video config again and a key frame (none yet, or the pipeline asks), bit 2 send the sound's config again. `detection` = the motion detection's sensitivity for that drone (0-3, as `DETECTION_SET`).
- **`OBSERVE_STATE [0x51][state u8][width u16][height u16][fps u8][observers u8]`** phone to PC, the answer to every OBSERVE (from the answer thread, never the receive thread) and once to each observer when the observation ends: 0 streaming, 1 refused - a pilot is connected (or was heard within 10 s, except for that same PC: its *back to the grid*), 2 ended - a pilot connected, 3 refused - 3 observers already, 4 refused - not in normal power mode (STANDBY, a sleep), 5 stopped (the answer to OBSERVE_STOP), 6 refused or ended - the flight controller isn't known DISARMED (unknown counts as armed).
- **`OBSERVE_STOP [0x52][key UTF]`** PC to phone, twice: this observer leaves. CONNECT on a tile sends it to every observed phone (the chosen one too) before the pilot's `Connect`.
- **The stream** goes from the phone's link port to each observer's address and port while observers are in its table (they expire 3 s after their last OBSERVE) and no pilot is connected: upstream's `VideoInitialFrame` (its number left 0; nothing is acknowledged), `KeyFrame`, `VideoFrame`, `VIDEO_PARITY_GROUP`, `AudioInitialFrame`, `AudioFrame` - the one encoder's packets copied to each, at the biggest picture any observer asks for, H.264 with a parity per group of 8 packets, key frames once. The `VideoInitialFrame` goes again ahead of every key frame. The sound follows the pc.34 gate with the observers' wish instead of the pilot's (`AUDIO`). A `TelemetryData` packet carries record 0x40FE five times a second and record 0x40FA (DETECTION) when an observer asked for it.
- **Record `0x40FE` OBSERVER**: `[flags u8][lat i32][lon i32, 1e-7 deg][alt i16 m][age s u16][fcHeading u16 0.1 deg, 65535 none][phoneAzimuth u16 0.1 deg, 65535 none][accuracy u8: Android's 0-3, 255 unknown][error u8 deg, 255 unknown][hfov u16 0.01 deg, 0 unknown]` (21 bytes after the code). Flags: bit 0 a position, 1 from the FC's GPS (else the phone's), 2 the FC has a compass (MSP status sensor bit 2: its yaw is a compass heading), 3 armed, 4 armed unknown, 5 the front camera. `phoneAzimuth` is where the camera looks by the phone's rotation vector sensor (the back camera along the device's -Z; the top edge when the phone lies flat), listened to only while observed.
- **The PC's heading choice**: the FC's compass when flag 2 and a heading are there, else the phone's azimuth; *approximate* while the FC's compass isn't known calibrated on this PC (`compass.calibrated.<phone>`, written by Drone setup when it reads `mag_calibration` with that phone chosen) or the phone's accuracy is under 2 (or unknown) or its error over 25 degrees.
- **Rules on the phone**: an OBSERVE is never a session - no stick frame, idle sticks or anything else of an observer's is taken; a pilot's `Connect` follows the takeover rules above unchanged and ends the observation for every observer at once (state 2), its `Config` and `VIDEO_PROFILE` setting the camera as usual. With the last observer gone the camera and encoder stop, as after a pilot's `Disconnect`.

## Telemetry / OSD (milestone 3)
- `TelemetryData` = a run of `[code int16][payload]` records with **no length field**. You must know every code's layout to read past it.
  The full decoder is `Control/.../Udp.processTelemetryData`, and the drawing code is `Control/.../Osd.java`.

## Motion detection (DRAUPNE Link 1.0.46 + DRAUPNE Flight 2.3.0-pc.35)
- PC to phone: `DETECTION_SET [0x7C][sensitivity u8: 0 off, 1 low, 2 normal, 3 high]`, unnumbered, once a second from the session thread (never the stick thread), only to a phone that reported pc.35 or newer (record 0x40F1; an older one would drop it anyway). The phone takes it as off 5 s after the last one.
- The phone analyses only while asked, the camera running and `FcArmedState` DISARMED (checked every 0.1 s; unknown counts as armed), from a second small YUV stream of the internal camera (an ImageReader at the video's shape, about 320 wide, in the capture session but in the repeating request only while analysing; a phone that refuses a session with it gets one without and says NO_PICTURE) or a USB camera's frame callback (NV21, set only while analysing). Never from the encoder's pictures. 8 pictures a second at most, on a background-priority thread; the detector is `Common/.../MotionDetector` (pure Java, unit-tested on the PC).
- Phone to PC: record `0x40FA` DETECTION above. The PC keeps each box by id and fades it out 1 s after the last record that had it (`MotionBoxes`); spectators get the records with the telemetry the pilot PC passes on.

## Overwatch sleep (DRAUPNE Link 1.0.47 + DRAUPNE Flight 2.3.0-pc.36)
- **The request:** `POWER_MODE [0x6E][3][checkIntervalMin u8][sensitivity u8: 1 low, 2 normal, 3 high][flags u8: bit 0 the clip records the microphone][token u32]`, unnumbered, repeated every 200 ms like the other modes until `PHONE_POWER` says mode 3; only to a phone that reported pc.36 or newer (an older one logs "no such mode" and stays as it is). The token is random, new with every request; the PC keeps it per drone (with the drone's position and heading then, for the map) in its settings. Refused, and left, while the flight controller isn't known to be disarmed, as DEEP SLEEP. After the 1 s countdown the phone sleeps as in deep sleep (FC port, GPS, link socket, telemetry, microphone, timer closed; screen locked) but keeps its partial wake lock and runs the camera with the motion detection's small picture alone (a capture session without the encoder: `InternalCamera.setWatchOnly`; a USB camera keeps its normal stream, its frames dropped). Its check windows, hellos and alarms are deep sleep's; `PHONE_POWER` caps bit 3 = the camera can give the analysis picture (overwatch possible).
- **Sleep hello (pc.36):** `[0x6F][battery][charging][nextCheckMin][mV u16][owFlags u8][floorReason u8]` - owFlags bit 0 overwatching, bit 1 an event runs; floorReason why overwatch gave way to deep sleep by itself (`PowerFloor`: 1 battery under 20 % and not charging, 2 thermal status SEVERE or worse - API 29 on - else the battery at 45 C or more; two readings 30 s apart). An older PC reads the first six bytes.
- **Outside the link:** the phone sends from a socket of its own (any port, never the link's) to the PC's last address (its link port); the PC's link threads (`ControlLink`'s receive thread, or `NameService`'s answer thread when no link holds the port) hand every such datagram to `OverwatchHub` as it is, and the hub answers from a socket of its own to the address it came from. Every packet starts `[code u8][token u32]`; the phone takes commands with its current token only, the PC packets of tokens it gave. Encoded and read by the shared `Common/.../OverwatchProtocol` (tests in both directions).
- `ALERT [0x7A]` phone to PC: `[event u16][alertNo u8][flags u8: bit 0 a light, 1 night, 2 again after a dismiss's 10 min][startedAt i64 wall ms][box x, y, w, h u16 of 65535][angle i16 0.01 deg right of the camera's axis][hfov u16 0.01 deg][lat i32][lon i32 1e-7 deg][posAge u16 s, 65535 none][battery u8 %, 255 unknown][name UTF]`, at once on a detection that travels (the 1.0.46 detector: not vegetation; lights at night), then every second until `CMD_ACK`.
- `COMMAND [0x7B]` PC to phone: `[event u16][cmd u8][args]` - 1 ACK (the alert is here: the phone sends the pre-roll), 2 PREVIEW (stream the live preview; every second while the card shows, off 3 s after the last), 3 DISMISS (no preview, no alert until the motion ends - or 10 min, then `ALERT` again with alertNo + 1), 4 CONNECT (wake fully: the PC connects as its pilot), 5 ENDED_ACK, 6 CLIP_GET `[offset u32][count u16]`, 7 CLIP_DONE `[ok u8]` (the clip is here and checked: the phone deletes it; from 1.0.49 then `[startedAt i64]`, the INFO's - an event's segments share its number, so the phone deletes exactly that segment; a phone reads the tail when it is there, and takes a DONE without it, from an older PC, for the clip it announced last), 8 STATUS_ACK, 9 PREROLL (the pre-roll again).
- `PREVIEW [0x7D]` phone to PC: `[event u16][unit u16][type u8][total u32][offset u32][bytes, 1024 at most]` - fragments of a unit: 0 a pre-roll JPEG `[capturedAt i64 wall ms][jpeg]` (the analysis picture, about 320 wide, two a second, the last 5 s before the alert: `PreRoll`), 1 the H.264 config (Annex B), 2 a key frame, 3 a frame. The preview is the stream encoder at the camera's size, H.264, 1 Mbit/s (step 3), in the session beside the event's recorder; sent only while the PC's PREVIEW is fresh, from a key frame on. `UnitAssembler` puts units together in any order and gives one up when 8 newer ones are open.
- `CLIP [0x7E]` phone to PC: `[event u16][type u8]` then 0 INFO `[size u32][sha256 32][durationMs u32][startedAt i64][flags u8: bit 0 sound][width u16][height u16]` (every 5 s while a clip waits and no chunk was asked for), 1 CHUNK `[offset u32][bytes, 1024 at most]`, 2 ENDED `[endedAt i64][clip u8: 1 a clip follows]` (the motion ended: 30 s without a detection; every second until ENDED_ACK, 2 min at most), 3 NONE `[why UTF]`. The PC asks for 64 chunks at a time (`ClipTransfer`), again from the first gap after 1.5 s, writes only the gapless part to a `.part` file (going on from its length after any stop), checks the SHA-256, and only then says CLIP_DONE. It asks nothing while a drone on that PC is armed; the phone serves nothing while its own flight controller isn't known to be disarmed. The clip: the phone's own H.264 encoder at the camera's size (0.1 bit a pixel at its frame rate, 3-12 Mbit/s) plus AAC 64 kbit/s mono when the request's sound flag is set, MP4, from the alert to the motion's end (15 min at most a clip).
- **Segments and the phone's clip folder (DRAUPNE Flight pc.38):** an event records in segments of 60 s (`ClipStore.SEGMENT_MS`; cut at the next key frame, the encoders and the camera's session untouched), each one a clip of its own: INFO goes on with `[part u16: 1 the first][eventStartedAt i64: the alert's startedAt]` (an older PC stops after height; an older phone sends no tail - read as part 0), and its `startedAt` is that segment's own start (the first segment's is the alert's, as before). A PC before 1.0.49 files segments 2 and later as events of their own (found by `startedAt`); nothing is lost. The phone keeps every clip on disk with its facts beside it (`<clip>.mp4.meta`), scans the folder at the app's start (a segment cut off mid-recording is kept only when its MP4 is whole), keeps the folder under 2 GB and 1 GB of the phone free (clips the PC has but couldn't be deleted first, then given up ones, then the oldest), and announces a new head clip at once (CLIP_GET names the event only: the phone serves only the clip it announced last). A clip sent whole but neither confirmed nor asked for within 20 s, or asked for from 0 again after it went whole (the PC's check failed), counts an attempt and goes to the back; after 5 it is given up (kept on the phone, no longer offered). Clips are hashed on a thread of their own.
- `STATUS [0x7F]` phone to PC: `[state u8: 2 dropped to deep sleep][reason u8][battery u8][tempDeci i16]`, five times a second apart or until STATUS_ACK.
- Telemetry records `0x40FB`-`0x40FD` are set aside for overwatch and not used yet.

## Spectator mode packets (PC to PC, codes 0x60-0x69 and 0x6D)
**Power modes (DRAUPNE Link 1.0.28 + DRAUPNE Flight pc.21):** the PC sends `PowerMode [0x6E][mode u8][checkIntervalMin u8]` (unnumbered, like `StartVideo`; older apps drop it) with mode 0 = normal, 1 = STANDBY (the phone stops its camera and encoder, slows its GPS to one fix per 30 s and its telemetry to 1 Hz, dims its screen; the link stays and no sticks are sent), 2 = DEEP SLEEP (the phone reports it with a countdown, then closes the flight controller's port, the GPS, the video and its UDP socket, locks its screen when it may, and sleeps; every `checkIntervalMin` minutes it listens 20 s for the PC's `Connect` and wakes fully on one). The PC repeats `PowerMode` every 200 ms until the phone's `0x40F5` PHONE_POWER record (once a second, and three times at a change) says the mode: `[mode u8][checkIntervalMin u8][sleepInS u16][caps u8]`, always the last record of its packet; caps bit 0 = the app may lock the screen (device admin), bit 1 = exact alarms allowed, bit 2 = battery optimisation off. An interval of 0 keeps the phone's current one (default 5 min). A request refused because the flight controller is armed is reported as mode 0.

**Sleep check (DRAUPNE Link 1.0.29 + DRAUPNE Flight pc.22):** in each check window the sleeping phone sends (pc.30: every 1.5 s through the window, until answered) `SleepHello [0x6F][battery % u8, 255 = unknown][charging u8][nextCheckMin u8][mV u16]` to the PC's last address, three times 500 ms apart; the PC answers `SleepAnswer [0x70][wake u8]` at once - 0 while it is dormant (the phone closes its window and sleeps on, instead of listening the whole 20 s), 1 after WAKE (the phone wakes fully, and the PC's `Connect`s resume). No answer: the window runs its 20 s as before. The LINK card shows the phone's battery and when it was last heard. While the phone sleeps the PC is *dormant*: it sends no `Connect` (one in a listening window would wake the phone) until WAKE. Both modes are refused, and left, while the flight controller reports armed.

**Answered with no link open (DRAUPNE Link 1.0.41):** after a DISCONNECT the PC's name service holds the link port and answers a sleeping phone's `SleepHello` itself: 0 (sleep on) until WAKE, which connects; the link then answers the next one 1.

See the table in `Spectate.java`. `PILOT_VIEW [0x76][version u8 = 1][flags u8][rcWaiting UTF][banner UTF][linkHint UTF][channels u8][u16 x n]` (1.0.41; followed by the phone it flies as a UTF) is sent by the pilot PC from its stream port every 250 ms, paused or not: what its own OSD warns of (flags: bit 0 link ready, 1 sticks live, 2 radio frozen, 3 arm switch not read, 4 relayed, 5 stalled, 6 its picture upside down); the spectator's OSD shows that instead of its own state. An older spectator drops it. `PAUSE [0x6D][paused u8]` is sent by the pilot PC every 0.5 s while it holds the stream (its link to the drone is slow) and once with 0 when it goes on; while paused the sequence stands still and packets thrown away at the pause are never resent.

**BYE with a reason (1.0.41):** `BYE [0x67][1]` from a pilot PC that disconnected on purpose (DISCONNECT, or the program closed); the spectator says the pilot left, not a link loss. An older spectator reads the first byte only.

**Where the drone is (DRAUPNE Flight pc.32, DRAUPNE Link 1.0.41):** the phone's `NAME_REPLY` goes on after the version with `[block version u8 = 1][source u8: 0 none, 1 the FC's GPS (2D/3D fix under 10 s old), 2 the phone's (under 2 min)][lat i32][lon i32, 1e-7 degrees][age s u16][armed u8: 0, 1, 2 = unknown][flown u8: a PC flies it][n u8][the pilot PC's address, n bytes]`. An older PC stops after the version.

**Remote update (DRAUPNE Link 1.0.41 + DRAUPNE Flight pc.32):** the PC sends `REMOTE_UPDATE [0x77][version UTF][APK url UTF][SHA-256 UTF]` (unnumbered, again each second until answered); the phone answers in its stats with record `0x40F7 PHONE_UPDATE [state u8: 1 downloading, 2 installing, 3 a tap needed on the phone, 4 failed, 5 refused - not disarmed, 6 already that version][percent u8][text UTF]`, twice a second while one runs and for 30 s after the last change, last in its packet. The phone takes it only for a newer version and with the flight controller known to be disarmed, checks the SHA-256, installs, and opens again by itself. **From pc.38 (H9):** the phone asks the flight controller before every 64 KB of the download and stops the moment it isn't a fresh DISARMED - the record then says 5 (refused - not disarmed) with "the download stopped" in its text; state 2 (installing) is set right before the APK goes to Android's installer (it was set after), and states 2 and 3 are reported for as long as they last (5 min at most), not only 30 s. The PC refuses to arm while the phone's latest `PHONE_UPDATE` says 2 or 3 and is still being reported (the install ends the phone app and the sticks with it).
